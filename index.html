      How to play

      1. 1Everyone gets the same secret word, except the imposter.
      2. 2Pass the phone. Tap the card, peek, tap to hide, pass it on.
      3. 3Go around in order and say one word related to the secret word.
      4. 4Vote. Catch the imposter and every crew member gets +1. Pick wrong and the imposter gets +2.
      5. 5A caught imposter can still steal the win by guessing the word.
